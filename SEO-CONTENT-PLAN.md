# MindCanopy SEO Content Strategy

## Competitive baseline

Amaha is the SEO content leader in India therapy. Their entire traffic engine is blog content, not product pages. One article ("how to control anger issues") drives 2,621 visits per month, 8% of their site traffic from a single page.

BetterLyf appears to have 40k traffic per month but around 70% comes from love-quotes-in-Telugu and similar quote pages, irrelevant to therapy buyers. Their real therapy traffic is around 10k per month.

YourDost has 14k traffic per month, 56% from people Googling "yourdost" by name. Non-brand SEO is thin. Beatable.

## The 8 content patterns that work in this market

Reverse-engineered from Amaha's top 40 pages.

1. **City pages.** `/therapist-in-mumbai`, `/centre/bengaluru`, etc. KD 17 to 45. 184 to 2,630 visits per month each. Highest ROI for MindCanopy because we have none yet.
2. **"X full form / X meaning".** One article ranks for huge volume. "OCD full form" (165k vol) ranks Amaha #4 for 2,158 visits per month. "Peniaphobia meaning" (14,800) ranks #2 for 1,410 visits per month.
3. **"How to control / deal with X".** "Anger issues" drives 2,621 per month.
4. **"Why do I X".** Confessional searches with high conversion intent. "Why do I feel tired", "why do I hate my life", "why is my husband yelling at me".
5. **"Difference between X and Y".** "Psychosis vs neurosis" 901 per month, "psychologist vs psychiatrist" 275 per month.
6. **"Stages of X".** "Stages of depression" ranks #1 for 1,000 per month.
7. **"Free X" intent capture.** One Amaha page ranks for 103 keywords, drives 1,330 per month from "free online therapy", "free counseling", etc.
8. **Zeitgeist / pop culture.** Situationship, symbiosexuals, neurodivergent, "best motivational Bollywood movies". Captures trending searches before competitors notice them.

## Where Amaha is weak (where MindCanopy can overtake)

- Clinical writing tone (textbook-y). MindCanopy's voice is sharper.
- Couples therapy page gets only 192 visits per month. Underinvested.
- Zero teen content in top 40 pages.
- No "therapy without family knowing", "in-laws marriage stress", "Indian family therapy" content. Culturally loaded searches with no competition.

## Article structure for the "Free online therapy" piece (highest leverage)

1. Honest opening. "Most articles about this are either lying or hiding something. Let's start with the truth."
2. What's actually free in India: iCall (9152987821), Vandrevala (1860-2662-345), AASRA, NIMHANS, university counselling, govt employee programs.
3. City tables: free centres in Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai.
4. What "free" usually means online: peer apps (Wysa, 7 Cups), trial bait, volunteer helplines, "first session free" gimmicks.
5. Demographic sections: teens, students, depression-specific.
6. Pivot to MindCanopy: "we're not free, here's why, but the first 15 min are."
7. FAQ section with schema markup (featured snippet bait).

## Strategic calls

1. City pages first (Tier 1, weeks 1 to 4). Fastest wins, lowest KD.
2. The "Free online therapy" piece is the highest single ROI article.
3. Couples is the long-game wedge. Tier 3 article #14 plus dedicated `/for/couples` upgrade.
4. Update top 10 articles quarterly to maintain freshness vs Amaha's stale content.
5. Each article ends with one specific CTA to `/questionnaire`, not multiple.
